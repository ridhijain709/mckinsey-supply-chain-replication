# Leadership Recommendations — Priority Action Stack

> Prioritized by EBITDA impact, feasibility, and time-to-realization. For full 30-60-90 execution plan, see [07-implementation-roadmap/30-60-90-plan.md](../07-implementation-roadmap/30-60-90-plan.md).

## Recommendation 1: Accelerate Swiggy One Penetration in Instamart
**Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL | **Timeline:** 0–90 days | **EBITDA Impact:** +₹38,200/store/month

**Action:** Run subscription conversion campaigns targeting repeat grocery buyers (≥2 Instamart orders in trailing 30 days). Current penetration ~8%; target 18% within 12 months.

**Business Logic:** Swiggy One subscribers order 2.1× more frequently and spend 38% more per order. At 18% penetration of a 320-order-day dark store, subscriber orders lift average orderbook AOV from ₹520 → ₹561 (+₹41), adding ₹38,200/month EBITDA.

**Metrics:** Subscriber conversion rate (%), subscriber AOV vs non-subscriber AOV, subscriber order frequency.

## Recommendation 2: Enforce 2.0–2.5km Radius Hard Cap on New Store Launches
**Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL | **Timeline:** 0–30 days (policy) | **EBITDA Impact:** +₹44,400–₹1,33,500/store/month

**Action:** Mandate maximum 2.5km delivery radius for all dark stores. For stores currently operating at 3.0–4.0km radius, conduct catchment compression analysis to identify whether demand is concentrated within the inner 2km zone.

**Business Logic:** Each 1km of additional delivery radius adds ₹7/order in delivery cost (rider fuel + time), eroding ₹44,400–₹1,33,500/month EBITDA at base volumes. Demand outside 2km typically represents <15% of orders but causes disproportionate cost inflation.

**Metrics:** Average delivery distance per store (km), delivery cost per order, on-time delivery rate.

## Recommendation 3: AOV Uplift Program — Bundle Curation & Substitution Engine
**Priority:** 🟡 HIGH | **Timeline:** 30–60 days | **EBITDA Impact:** +₹50,000–₹100,000/store/month

**Action:** Launch "Smart Basket" algorithm that recommends complementary items at checkout, and introduces minimum order value incentives (e.g., free delivery above ₹499 vs current ₹399 threshold).

**Business Logic:** A ₹100 AOV increase yields ₹18.5 additional gross revenue per order at 18.5% blended take rate. At 320 orders/day, this adds ₹1,68,960/month gross revenue. After platform subsidies and discounts (est. 40% haircut), net EBITDA add is ~₹1,01,376/month.

**Metrics:** Average basket size (₹), attach rate of recommended items (%), conversion on minimum order nudge.

## Recommendation 4: Dark Store Profitability Triage — Density Intervention for 200–320 ord/day Stores
**Priority:** 🟡 HIGH | **Timeline:** 30–90 days | **EBITDA Impact:** Network-level ₹50–₹200 Crore/year

**Action:** Classify all 600+ dark stores into 3 tiers based on current order density:
- **Tier A (>380 ord/day):** Scale resources; these are self-sustaining.
- **Tier B (200–380 ord/day):** Targeted demand densification campaigns (hyperlocal ads, Society partnerships, WhatsApp dark store group pilots).
- **Tier C (<200 ord/day):** Evaluate for consolidation or 60-day intervention program before closure decision.

**Business Logic:** Moving just 100 Tier B stores from 250 → 350 orders/day improves EBITDA by ₹67,800/store/month = **₹6.78 Crore/month network-wide**.

**Metrics:** Store-level order density (orders/day), % stores above break-even threshold, store EBITDA distribution.

---

## Recommendation 5: Rider Dispatch Algorithm Upgrade — Zone-Level Batching
**Priority:** 🟢 MEDIUM | **Timeline:** 60–90 days | **EBITDA Impact:** –₹3–₹7/order delivery cost reduction

**Action:** Implement demand-weighted rider zone assignment that batches co-directional orders (2-order batching target: 35% of all orders). Current 1.2× batch ratio should be improved to 1.5×.

**Business Logic:** Zone-batching at 1.5× reduces effective delivery cost per order by ~₹5–₹8. At 320 orders/day, this saves ₹48,000–₹76,800/month. Technology investment payback period: <3 months.

**Metrics:** Average orders per rider trip, delivery cost per order, on-time delivery rate (must stay ≥88%).
