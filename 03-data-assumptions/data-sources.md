## Data Limitations

- Swiggy does not disclose per-store EBITDA publicly; all store-level figures are modelled estimates using disclosed aggregate economics
- Rider cost estimates are based on public gig worker earnings data and industry averages — actual figures vary by city tier and demand cycle
- AOV distributions reflect industry averages; individual store AOV may vary significantly by location demographics
