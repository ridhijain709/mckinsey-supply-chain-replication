# 30-60-90 Day Implementation Roadmap

> Phased execution plan for Swiggy Instamart dark-store profitability optimization.  
> Owner: Business Operations + Analytics | Sponsor: Instamart VP Operations

---

## PHASE 1: Foundations (Days 0–30)
**Theme:** Stop the bleeding — enforce structural constraints that cost nothing to implement but yield immediate EBITDA uplift.

### Week 1–2: Policy & Measurement Setup

| # | Action | Owner | Success Metric | EBITDA Impact |
|:-:|:-------|:-----:|:--------------:|:-------------:|
| 1 | Mandate 2.5km delivery radius hard cap for all stores | Ops | 100% store compliance | +₹44–₹133K/store/month |
| 2 | Deploy store-level EBITDA dashboard (break-even tracker) | Analytics | Live for 100% of stores | Visibility |
| 3 | Classify all stores into Tier A/B/C by order density | Strategy | Classification complete | Foundation |
| 4 | Safety-stock tiering: Top 50 SKUs → 2-day buffer | Procurement | Fill rate tracking started | –Stockout risk |
| 5 | Freeze new dark store launches pending density audit | Leadership | Moratorium in place | Capital efficiency |

### Week 3–4: Quick Wins

| # | Action | Owner | Success Metric | EBITDA Impact |
|:-:|:-------|:-----:|:--------------:|:-------------:|
| 6 | Launch Swiggy One upsell at Instamart checkout | Growth | CVR ≥8% on upsell nudge | +₹38K/store/month |
| 7 | Raise minimum order value: ₹399 → ₹449 (test) | Product | AOV change tracking | +₹14K/store/month |
| 8 | Rider zone rebalancing for top-10 over-radius stores | Rider Ops | Delivery cost ↓ | +₹15–₹30K/store |
| 9 | FIFO enforcement protocol at dark store level | Ops | Return rate measurement | –₹8K/store/month loss |

**Phase 1 Target:** 100% of Tier A stores above break-even. 60% of Tier B stores improving trajectory.
